#define PRINCE_ENC_ENABLE_EN ( x)

EN - Enables PRINCE encryption for flash programming. 0b0..Encryption of writes to the flash controller DATAW* registers is disabled. 0b1..Encryption of writes to the flash controller DATAW* registers is enabled. Reading of PRINCE-encrypted flash regions is disabled.
